Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a moka pot and an espresso machine?

An Italian moka pot works at low pressure (1–2 bar) and produces a strong, full-bodied coffee. An espresso machine uses high pressure (9+ bar) for a true espresso with crema. More details in our comparison.

Which moka pot is best for beginners?

A 3-cup model like the Bialetti Fiammetta or Moka Express. Affordable, reliable and ideal to start with. See our buying guide.

How much does a good Italian moka pot cost?

From £20–25 for entry-level models. The best value for money is between £30 and £50. Premium design (Alessi Pulcina) costs £80–120.

Does an aluminium moka pot work on induction?

No. Induction only works with magnetic materials. Choose a stainless steel model like the Bialetti Venus.

What coffee grind should I use?

A medium grind between filter and espresso — often called "moka grind". Too fine causes clogging, too coarse gives weak coffee.

How long does an Italian moka pot last?

10 to 20 years with proper care. Only the gasket and handle wear out — replace the gasket once a year.
