Frequently asked questions about moka pots

We often receive the same questions from readers discovering slow coffee. Here are the answers to the most common ones — concise, clear and jargon-free.

What is the difference between a moka pot and an espresso machine?

A moka pot uses steam pressure to push water through the coffee, while an espresso machine uses much higher pressure. Moka pots are simpler, cheaper and produce a milder coffee. Espresso machines make a more intense coffee and can make milk-based drinks, but are more complex and expensive.

Which material is better: aluminium or stainless steel?

Both have advantages. Aluminium is lighter, cheaper and heats faster, but doesn't work on induction. Stainless steel is more durable, induction-compatible and easier to clean, but more expensive and heavier. For taste, the difference is minimal.

How often should I clean my moka pot?

Rinse with hot water after every use. Full clean with disassembly weekly. Monthly descaling with vinegar. Never use soap or washing-up liquid.

What size moka pot should I choose?

Choose based on your daily consumption: 3-cup for 1–2 people, 6-cup for families. Moka pots work best when filled to the brim, so don't go too large.
